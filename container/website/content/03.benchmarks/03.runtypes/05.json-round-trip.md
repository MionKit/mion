Each chart is the geometric mean over every case in that group. A strategy that does not support a case scores it at the worst result among the strategies that do. The link on each chart opens those cases on GitHub.

Speed is the encode pass, with decode after the dot. Bytes are the encoded payload, where fewer is better.
